Best Recipes with Tofu: Six Dishes Worth Making

These are the tofu recipes our invented test kitchen returns to again and again. They cover weeknight dinners, breakfast, and dessert, and each one shows off a different side of tofu.

1. Crispy Sesame Tofu Stir-Fry

Serves 3 to 4 · 30 minutes · firm or extra-firm tofu

You need: 1 block pressed tofu, 2 tablespoons cornstarch, 3 tablespoons oil, 3 cups mixed vegetables (broccoli, bell pepper, snap peas), 2 cloves garlic, 1 tablespoon grated ginger, 3 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup, 1 tablespoon rice vinegar, 1 teaspoon sesame oil, sesame seeds.

  1. Cube the tofu, toss with cornstarch, and pan-fry in 2 tablespoons oil until golden on all sides, about 8 minutes. Set aside.
  2. Stir-fry the vegetables in the remaining oil for 4 minutes. Add garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
  3. Whisk the soy sauce, honey, vinegar, and sesame oil. Pour into the pan, return the tofu, and toss until glazed.
  4. Top with sesame seeds and serve over rice.

2. Weekend Tofu Scramble

Serves 2 · 15 minutes · medium or firm tofu

You need: 1 block tofu, 1 tablespoon oil, 1/4 cup diced onion, 1/2 teaspoon turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon cumin, 1 tablespoon nutritional yeast, salt, a handful of spinach, 1 diced tomato.

  1. Sauté the onion in oil for 3 minutes.
  2. Crumble in the tofu with your hands. Add turmeric, cumin, nutritional yeast, and salt.
  3. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring, until most of the moisture is gone.
  4. Fold in the spinach and tomato for the last minute. Serve with toast.

3. Mapo-Style Silken Tofu

Serves 3 · 25 minutes · soft or silken tofu

You need: 1 block soft tofu, 2 tablespoons oil, 1/2 cup ground pork or crumbled mushrooms, 1 tablespoon chili bean paste, 2 cloves garlic, 1 cup broth, 1 tablespoon soy sauce, 1 teaspoon cornstarch mixed with 1 tablespoon water, sliced scallions.

  1. Brown the pork or mushrooms in oil. Stir in the chili paste and garlic for 1 minute.
  2. Add broth and soy sauce and bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Slide in the tofu in large cubes. Simmer for 5 minutes without stirring hard.
  4. Stir in the cornstarch mixture until the sauce thickens. Top with scallions and serve over rice.

4. Baked Teriyaki Tofu Bowls

Serves 4 · 40 minutes · extra-firm tofu

You need: 1 block pressed tofu, 1/3 cup teriyaki sauce, 1 tablespoon oil, cooked rice, steamed edamame, shredded carrot, sliced cucumber.

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Cut the tofu into slabs or cubes and toss with oil and half the teriyaki sauce.
  2. Bake on a lined tray for 25 to 30 minutes, flipping once, until the edges are chewy and browned.
  3. Brush with the remaining sauce. Serve over rice with the vegetables.

5. Creamy Tofu Chocolate Mousse

Serves 4 · 10 minutes plus chilling · silken tofu

You need: 1 block silken tofu, 6 ounces dark chocolate, 2 tablespoons maple syrup, 1 teaspoon vanilla, pinch of salt.

  1. Melt the chocolate gently in a microwave or over a double boiler and let it cool for 5 minutes.
  2. Blend the silken tofu with the maple syrup, vanilla, and salt until perfectly smooth.
  3. Add the chocolate and blend again. Spoon into cups and chill for at least 2 hours.

6. Tofu Banh Mi Sandwich

Serves 2 · 25 minutes · firm tofu

You need: 1/2 block pressed tofu, 2 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 teaspoon lime juice, 1 tablespoon oil, 2 baguette rolls, mayonnaise, quick-pickled carrot and radish, cilantro, sliced jalapeño.

  1. Slice the tofu into thin planks and marinate in soy sauce and lime juice for 10 minutes.
  2. Pan-fry in oil for 3 to 4 minutes per side until browned.
  3. Spread mayonnaise on the rolls and layer the tofu, pickled vegetables, cilantro, and jalapeño.

Tips for Better Results with Any Recipe

  • Press firm tofu before frying, baking, or air frying.
  • Never press silken tofu. Drain it gently and handle it like a delicate custard.
  • Add the sauce at the end of cooking so the tofu stays crisp.
  • Season at every stage, not only at the end.
