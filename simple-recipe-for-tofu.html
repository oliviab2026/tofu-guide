Simple Recipe for Tofu: Golden Pan-Fried Tofu

If you want one simple recipe for tofu that works every time, this is it. Five ingredients, one pan, and about 20 minutes give you golden, savory tofu that is crisp on the outside and tender in the middle.

Serves 2 to 3 · Prep 5 minutes plus pressing · Cook 12 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 block (14 to 16 oz) firm or extra-firm tofu
  • 2 tablespoons neutral oil, such as canola or avocado
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce or tamari
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon maple syrup or honey
  • Pinch of salt and black pepper

Instructions

  1. Press the tofu. Wrap the block in a clean towel and set a heavy pan on top for 15 to 20 minutes. This is the one step that makes the biggest difference.
  2. Slice. Cut the block into 8 rectangular slabs about 1/2 inch thick, or into cubes if you prefer. Pat the pieces dry and sprinkle with salt and pepper.
  3. Mix the glaze. Stir the soy sauce, garlic, and maple syrup together in a small bowl.
  4. Fry. Heat the oil in a nonstick or cast iron skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tofu in a single layer. Cook 4 to 5 minutes without moving it, until golden and easy to lift.
  5. Flip. Turn each piece and cook another 3 to 4 minutes on the second side.
  6. Glaze. Lower the heat to medium. Pour the glaze into the pan and turn the tofu for about 30 seconds, until the sauce clings and turns glossy. Serve right away.

Easy Variations

Lemon pepper

Skip the maple syrup and finish with lemon juice, zest, and extra black pepper.

Spicy

Add 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes or a teaspoon of chili garlic sauce to the glaze.

Sesame

Add 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil to the glaze and sprinkle sesame seeds on top.

What to Serve It With

  • Steamed rice and a green vegetable such as broccoli or bok choy
  • Noodles tossed with a little sesame oil
  • A big salad, with the tofu sliced over the top
  • A sandwich or wrap with crunchy vegetables

Tips for Success

  • Dry tofu browns better, so pat each piece with a towel before it hits the pan.
  • Do not crowd the skillet. Cook in two batches if the pieces touch.
  • Wait for the tofu to release on its own before flipping.
  • Add the glaze at the end. Sugar in a sauce burns quickly over high heat.

Storing and Reheating

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to four days. Reheat in a hot skillet or in the air fryer for 3 to 4 minutes to bring the crispness back. The microwave works but softens the outside.
