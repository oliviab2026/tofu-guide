Easy Quick Tofu Recipes: Five Dinners in 20 Minutes or Less

Tofu cooks fast because it is already cooked when you buy it. That makes it a great weeknight ingredient. These easy quick tofu recipes need minimal chopping, common pantry items, and one or two pans.

1. Five-Minute Soy Scallion Silken Tofu

Serves 2 · 5 minutes · no cooking

You need: 1 block silken tofu, 2 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 teaspoon sesame oil, 1 teaspoon chili oil (optional), 2 sliced scallions.

  1. Drain the tofu gently and slide it onto a plate. Cut into squares.
  2. Spoon the soy sauce, sesame oil, and chili oil over the top.
  3. Scatter the scallions and serve cold with rice.

2. Speedy Peanut Tofu Noodles

Serves 2 · 15 minutes · firm tofu

You need: half a block of firm tofu, 6 ounces noodles, 2 tablespoons peanut butter, 1 tablespoon soy sauce, 1 tablespoon lime juice, 1 teaspoon chili sauce, 2 tablespoons warm water, shredded carrot, chopped peanuts.

  1. Boil the noodles according to the package while you whisk the peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, chili sauce, and warm water into a smooth sauce.
  2. Cube the tofu and sear in a dry nonstick pan for 5 minutes until lightly browned.
  3. Toss the drained noodles with the sauce, carrot, and tofu. Top with peanuts.

3. Tofu Fried Rice

Serves 3 · 15 minutes · firm tofu

You need: 1/2 block firm tofu, 2 cups cold cooked rice, 1 cup frozen peas and carrots, 2 tablespoons oil, 2 cloves garlic, 2 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 teaspoon sesame oil, 2 scallions.

  1. Crumble the tofu and cook in 1 tablespoon oil over high heat for 5 minutes until the edges brown. Push to one side.
  2. Add the remaining oil, garlic, and frozen vegetables. Cook 2 minutes.
  3. Add the rice and soy sauce and stir-fry 4 minutes, breaking up clumps.
  4. Finish with sesame oil and scallions.

4. Tofu Tacos with Quick Taco Crumble

Serves 3 · 15 minutes · extra-firm tofu

You need: 1 block extra-firm tofu, 1 tablespoon oil, 1 tablespoon chili powder, 1 teaspoon cumin, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, 1 tablespoon soy sauce, 6 small tortillas, shredded cabbage, salsa, lime wedges.

  1. Crumble the tofu into a hot oiled pan and cook 6 to 8 minutes until the moisture is gone.
  2. Stir in the spices and soy sauce and cook 2 more minutes.
  3. Warm the tortillas and fill with the crumble, cabbage, and salsa. Serve with lime.

5. Miso Tofu Soup

Serves 2 · 10 minutes · soft tofu

You need: 3 cups water or vegetable broth, 2 tablespoons miso paste, 1/2 block soft tofu in small cubes, a handful of spinach or sliced mushrooms, 1 sliced scallion.

  1. Heat the water or broth until steaming, not boiling.
  2. Add the mushrooms for 3 minutes if using, then the tofu and spinach for 1 minute.
  3. Turn off the heat. Stir the miso in a ladle of hot liquid until smooth, then return it to the pot. Top with scallion.

Pantry Staples for Fast Tofu Cooking

  • Soy sauce or tamari
  • Toasted sesame oil
  • Garlic and ginger (fresh or jarred)
  • Chili sauce or chili flakes
  • Rice vinegar or limes
  • Cornstarch for a quick crisp coating
  • Frozen vegetables and cooked rice

Time-Saving Tips

  • Buy tofu that is already pressed or choose baked tofu when you are truly short on time.
  • Press while you prep other ingredients. Ten minutes still helps.
  • Crumbled tofu browns faster than cubes because more surface touches the pan.
  • Press and cube a whole block on Sunday and keep it in the fridge for the week.
