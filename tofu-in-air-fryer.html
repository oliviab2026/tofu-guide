Tofu in Air Fryer: Crispy Cubes in 15 Minutes

An air fryer is one of the easiest ways to get crisp, golden tofu with very little oil. Our test kitchen has cooked hundreds of batches, and this method works in basket and oven-style models alike.

What You Need

  • 1 block (14 to 16 oz) extra-firm or firm tofu
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil, such as avocado or canola
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Black pepper to taste

Step by Step

  1. Press the tofu. Wrap the block in a clean kitchen towel, set a heavy pan on top, and press for 20 to 30 minutes. Less water means a crispier result.
  2. Cut it. Slice into 3/4-inch cubes. Smaller pieces get crunchier, while larger ones stay softer inside.
  3. Season. Toss the cubes gently with the soy sauce and oil. Let them sit for 5 minutes.
  4. Coat. Sprinkle on the cornstarch, garlic powder, paprika, and pepper. Toss until each cube has a light, even dusting.
  5. Preheat. Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C) for 3 minutes if your model has a preheat setting.
  6. Cook. Arrange the cubes in a single layer with space between them. Cook for 12 to 15 minutes, shaking the basket or flipping the pieces at the halfway point.
  7. Check. The tofu is done when the edges are deep golden and the cubes feel firm and crisp when tapped. Add 2 to 3 minutes if needed.

Time and Temperature Guide

Small cubes (1/2 inch)

390°F for 10 to 12 minutes. Very crunchy.

Medium cubes (3/4 to 1 inch)

400°F for 12 to 15 minutes. Crisp outside, chewy inside. Our favorite.

Slabs or planks (1/2 inch thick)

375°F for 14 to 18 minutes, flipping once. Great for sandwiches.

Flavor Variations

  • Sesame ginger: Swap the paprika for 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger and toss with toasted sesame seeds after cooking.
  • Buffalo style: Cook plain, then toss in hot sauce mixed with a little melted butter or vegan butter.
  • Taco spiced: Use chili powder, cumin, and a pinch of oregano in place of the paprika and garlic powder.
  • Sweet chili: Glaze the finished cubes with sweet chili sauce for the last minute of cooking.

Fixing Common Problems

My tofu is soggy

The block was probably not pressed long enough, or the basket was crowded. Press longer and cook in two batches.

The coating is falling off

Dry the cubes before adding cornstarch, and shake the basket gently rather than stirring hard.

It tastes bland

Tofu needs strong seasoning. Add more soy sauce to the soak, and serve it with a dipping sauce like peanut sauce or garlic chili sauce.

It is burning on the outside but soft inside

Lower the temperature by 25°F and cut the cubes a little smaller.

Ways to Serve It

Pile the crispy cubes on rice bowls, toss them into salads, tuck them into wraps, or serve them as a snack with dipping sauce. Leftovers keep in the fridge for three days and re-crisp in the air fryer at 375°F for 3 to 4 minutes.
