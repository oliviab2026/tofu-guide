Your Friendly Guide to Tofu: Types, Techniques, and Recipes

Welcome to Tender Bean Tofu Kitchen. If tofu has ever seemed mysterious, mushy, or boring, you are in the right place. We explain what tofu is, how to choose it, and how to cook it so it comes out crisp, creamy, or tender on purpose.

Tofu is made from soybeans that are turned into soy milk, curdled, and pressed into blocks. Its mild flavor is its biggest strength, because it takes on whatever sauce, spice, or smoke you give it. Pick the right type, prepare it well, and season it boldly, and tofu becomes one of the most flexible ingredients in your kitchen.

The Types of Tofu

The grocery store shelf can be confusing. Here is a quick guide to what you will see.

Silken tofu

Smooth and custardy, sold in shelf-stable boxes or tubs. Best for smoothies, creamy sauces, desserts, and soups. It breaks apart easily, so handle it gently.

Soft and medium tofu

Slightly more structure than silken. Good for gentle simmering in broths, mapo-style dishes, and scrambles with a tender texture.

Firm and extra-firm tofu

The everyday choice for frying, baking, grilling, and air frying. It holds its shape and crisps well, especially after pressing.

Smoked and baked tofu

Already seasoned and sturdy. Slice it straight from the package into sandwiches, salads, and grain bowls.

Fried tofu puffs

Golden, airy pockets that soak up soup and sauce. Common in noodle dishes and stews.

Tofu skin (yuba) and frozen tofu

Yuba is the delicate film that forms on heated soy milk and gets used in wraps and stir-fries. Frozen and thawed tofu turns chewy and spongy, which is great for stews and marinades.

Five Tips Before You Cook

  1. Match the type to the job: firm for crisping, silken for blending.
  2. Press firm tofu for 15 to 30 minutes so it can brown instead of steam.
  3. Season generously. Tofu is mild and needs salt, acid, and spice.
  4. Use high heat for crispness and low heat for tenderness.
  5. Store leftovers submerged in fresh water in the fridge and use within three to four days.
